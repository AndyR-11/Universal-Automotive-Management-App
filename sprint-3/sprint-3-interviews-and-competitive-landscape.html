Interview 1 Script

Goal: Learn how people use their car manufacturer's app for maintenance, and where it falls short.

Opening (1 min)

  • We tell them it's a class project about car maintenance apps.
  • Say there are no right or wrong answers.
  • Ask permission to take notes.

Screener

QuestionAnswer
Which car brand do you own, and which manufacturer app do you use?Owns a Hyundai Elantra. Uses the myHyundai app.
How long have you used the app?Used the app for one year with a free trial

Part 1: How they use it (3 min)

QuestionAnswer
1. Walk me through the last time you opened the app. What were you trying to do?I was just trying to remotely start my car.
2. What do you mainly use it for?Just the remote starting mainly, I didn't use the other features too often.
3. How often do you open it in a typical month?I usually opened it twice a day.
4. Does it cost anything to use it?I had a year free trail, but it would cost maybe 80 a year.
5. Did you try anything else before this?I had a Ford, so I used the Ford app which was free.

Part 2: Maintenance features (5 min)

QuestionAnswer
6. Does the app tell you when maintenance is due? How (notification, email, text)?Yes, but I think you would have to check yourself. There were also no available appointments when I checked.
7. Has it ever reminded you too late, too often, or not at all?I don't think it would remind, just recommended off mileage.
8. Can you see your past service history in the app? Is it complete? (Does it include work done at other shops or by you?)Yes, but it only shows work done at the dealership. It also doesn’t show the cost.
9. Do you track what you spend on your car anywhere? Is it in this app?No I don’t

Part 3: Gaps and frustrations (3 min)

QuestionAnswer
10. What is the most annoying or missing thing about the app?The appointment setting never worked so you had to call anyway to make an appointment.
11. What do you do when the app can't help (calendar, notes, receipts, asking the dealer)?Call for appointments
12. If you bought a car from a different brand, or owned two brands, what would you do with your records?Just store everything like the maintenance receipts in the glovebox.

Closing (2 min)

QuestionAnswer
13. If you could add one feature to the app, what would it be?Something to track how much you’ve spent on maintenance.
14. What would make you leave this app?The charge is what made me leave the app.

Teardown

The myHyundai app has many features, some related to our project and some not. Remote start/stop, door controls and the Bluelink features are out of scope. They also require an active Bluelink subscription, however, the app is free to download. The related features are a Car Care page, where you can see vehicle health and maintenance information, and a monthly vehicle health report. You can also see tire pressure, view recall status, and make a Car Care service appointment. The app tells you how many miles until your next service is due and shows service history (only for dealership work). The Hyundai app can read vehicle data directly from the car. Our app will be universal, allow you to log services from any shop, and give you the option to log costs as well.

Teardown

The Toyota app has features related and unrelated to our project. Remote Connect (remote start and lock) is out of scope. The related features are a Vehicle Health Report (recalls, warnings, maintenance info) and a Vehicle Diagnostic Data card. There are also maintenance reminders by email, in the app, or through your My Toyota account. There is an opt-in Vehicle Maintenance Alert that notifies you and your preferred dealer. You can schedule a service and view your service history (only for dealership work). Service Connect needs an active trial or paid subscription, and the app supports 2010 and newer models. Our app will be universal, allow you to log services from any shop, and give you the option to log costs as well.

Teardown

The Nissan app, named myNissan, has multiple features, some related and some non-related to our project. For example, you can remotely start/stop the vehicle, lock/unlock the doors, and control the a/c, all of which are out of scope for our project. The app also has a vehicle health feature where you can see information such as warning lights and tire pressure. You can also see when your next oil change is due, schedule a service on the app, view upcoming appointments, and view your service history (only for dealership service). The Nissan app has the advantage of being able to automatically update mileage and show warning lights, while our app will be universal, allow you to log services from any shop, and give you the option to log costs as well.

FieldEntry

TriggerPeople say they are comfortable using what they use now.
Proposed ownerProduct lead
MitigationMake logging one tap/simple.

FieldEntry
