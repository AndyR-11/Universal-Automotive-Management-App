Incident review

SectionReview
1. SummaryThe estimation worksheet and one-page budget were initially built based on the capacity of a student team working on the project. The project assumes full-time work, so we corrected it to 80 hours per person per sprint and every number that depends on it was recalculated.
2. ImpactVelocity, sprints needed, effort hours, FTE-months, labor cost and contingency had to be recalculated in two documents. The budget request is now $45,657. We spent around an hour redoing the work.
3. Timeline
  • 9/29: worksheet completed at 40 hours per person per sprint.
  • 10/1: mistake mentioned and identified in class.
  • 10/2: team confirmed the full-time basis of 80 hours.
  • 10/4: both documents correctly updated.
4. Contributing causesThe capacity assumption was never clarified with the professor before estimating. We did not notice the mistake until it was mentioned in class, so the mismatch was not caught until late.
5. What went wellThe mismatch was found before the final submission. The recalculation reused one set of formulas. We recorded this change in the change log.
