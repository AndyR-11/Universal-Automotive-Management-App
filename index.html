Every oil change, every mile, in one place.

Many vehicle owners track their maintenance across paper receipts, shop portals, and memory, which leads to missed services and surprise repairs. The Universal Automotive Management App gives drivers one place to track maintenance, log expenses, keep a full service history, and stay ahead of what their vehicle needs next.

What the app does

Maintenance tracking

Log every service and repair against each vehicle, from oil changes to major work.

Expense logging

Record what upkeep actually costs, and see spending build up over time.

Service history

A running record of everything done to a vehicle, kept in one place.

Upcoming reminders

Know what maintenance is coming up before it becomes an emergency.
