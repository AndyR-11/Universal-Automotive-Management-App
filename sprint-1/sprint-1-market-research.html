Aggregate findings

When we started researching, we had come up with a few different ideas for our project. As we did more and more interviews we slowly realized that automotive issues were some of the most common problems. Not to mention a lot of the people that we interviewed all had a lot of things in common. They all relied on mileage, warning lights, dealership reminders or just memory to take their car to get fixed. We also noticed that a lot of people all use AI and things like that to fix their car along with almost no one keeping track of how much they spend on their car maintenance. What we took away from our research was that drivers often lack a proper way of keeping track of when their next maintenance will be and how much they have spent, or what is coming up for their car etc. Even for us we believe this is a project that could help out every driver out there whether it is older or younger people.

Top pick, deep dive

As we talked about earlier, having one place to track car maintenance along with how much we’ve spent etc. would be a thing that could hep out a lot of drivers. This idea rose tot eh top of our list since in both the first and second round of interviews a lot of users stated the same issues which made us realize that this project could actually be a game changer. We heard a lot of waiting until a light flashes on and delaying maintenance due to school or forgetting to schedule an appointment. In both sets of interviews there were also quite a few “having everything together would be nice” which is also something that really stood out and caught our eyes. It is because of this that we decided on making a dashboard where the users can keep track of everything this way it helps the public be more organized and better situated as to how their car is looking

What didn't work / what you pivoted from

What we would say did not work from the other ideas was the lack on consistency within the answers. After looking over everything We were pretty shocked at how much overlap there was withing the automotive issues. Yes, there were a few overlaps here and there with the other ideas we had but it just seemed like automotive was pretty similar for every user we interviewed. We were honestly not expecting this since we are aware of a few brands that have something similar. We realized that yes some other issues with out other ideas were issued that not everyone has. This is the reason that the other ideas never really became a concrete option for our project.
