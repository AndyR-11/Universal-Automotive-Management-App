The Strategy-to-Project Chain

It doesn't matter whether you're a student, blue-collar worker, or office worker, vehicle owners often forget maintenance or have a hard time keeping track of their service history. This could lead to expensive repairs and a decrease in their vehicle's lifespan.

Our strategy is to simplify vehicle maintenance by building an application that tracks mileage and service history, informs owners of the costs of each repair and upcoming maintenance, while providing recommendations for repair shops, compatible parts, and do-it-yourself solutions.

Our business objective is to help vehicle owners stay ahead of maintenance, reduce unexpected repair costs, and make maintaining a vehicle easier and more convenient.

This project should exist because our application will bring maintenance tracking, reminders, service recommendations, and repair information into one place rather than using multiple websites or apps.

Problem → Strategy → Objective → Project

Missed maintenance → Simplify and centralize maintenance → Keep vehicles properly maintained → All-in-one maintenance app
