Step 4 — Compare

1. Hidden Work Comparison

T and D are the ones that appear most often in our work. Our similar products also dealt with the data related challenges. AUSTOsist was created with the intention of making vehicle maintenance info organized and CARFAX benefited from partnering with some service shops so that the vehicle history could automatically be added instead of having to do that manually. This backs up our belief of the importance of properly collecting and storing vehicle data in our project.

2. First Release Comparison

From what we saw AUTOsist took around a year to reach its first release with only 1 person while CARFAX took somewhere around a year and a half but we do not know what the team size was just like Drivvo. After researching these products, the lesson we took out of it was that we should focus on getting the most important features correct instead of trying to get everything done at once. We believe our project could take around 10-15 months to get it done!

3. Practice to Copy and Mistake to Avoid

For these similar products’ first releases, they stayed narrow with the functionality, they only did what they needed to which is what we will copy. For the mistake we will avoid what Drivvo did and make sure our app can be used by people internationally.

Comparable Estimate

Our comparables suggest a first release would take 10 to 15 months with a team of 4.
