Step 5 — Compare your two estimates

Story points gave a middle value of 464 hours, and the analogous method gave 664 hours — a gap of about 1.4. The two are decently close, and they start from different information, so the agreement is meaningful. The largest uncertainty is the fraction of AUTOsist's release that matches our slice, which is our judgment based on feature scope. The second uncertainty is our chosen velocity, which is unvalidated since we haven't run any sprints yet.
